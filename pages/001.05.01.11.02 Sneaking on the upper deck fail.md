- You slowly climb the staircase towards the upper deck. Rain pours down the open hatch and you suddenly slip on the wet staircase. You hit the floor with a loud bang and all attention sems to be on you. You quickly get a grip of the situation. The whole upper deck is completey infested with Meatbug eggs that might hatch any moment now. On the lookout farther away from your current position are a couple of undead magisters ready to rain down destruction and death upon you. At the steering wheel a whole infestation of undead Magisters, already hatched Meatbugs and Meatbug eggs has taken up position and is ready to fight you. At the bow of the ship a couple of undead rotten corpses and more Meatbug eggs await your arrival. Next to you on top of the lookout a couple of Magisters that are very much alive at this current point in time seem to hide from the monstrous creatures below them. Once they notice you they start readying themselves and shout in your direction against the storm: "Please help us off this ship! We have your back! Just give us a target and we will help you! We just want to survive!"
